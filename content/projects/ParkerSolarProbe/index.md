NASA's **Parker Solar Probe** flies closer to the Sun than any spacecraft before it. Its **IS⊙IS** suite measures energetic particles; Caltech leads the high-energy part, **EPI-Hi**.

## My role
Postdoctoral member of the EPI-Hi team at Caltech since 2023, working with Dr. Christina Cohen. I analyse SEP events seen by PSP and combine them with Solar Orbiter, STEREO and ACE to follow particles from near the Sun to 1 au. This work is part of the team recognised with a **NASA Group Achievement Award**.

## Key results
- **Inverse-velocity-arrival (IVA) events** — a catalogue of 14 SEP events where mid-energy particles arrive first, probing shock acceleration near the Sun (first author, [A&A 2026](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202660274); invited talk at AGU 2025).
- **Composition variation in the 2023 May 16 SEP event** seen by both Solar Orbiter and PSP (first author, [ApJL 2024](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ad8b18)).
- Co-author of studies on radial dependence of SEP fluences, Fe-rich multi-spacecraft events, shock properties of IVA events, and **deep-learning SEP prediction with PSP/IS⊙IS data** (JGR: Machine Learning and Computation, 2026).
