NASA's **Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe (IMAP)** studies particle acceleration throughout the heliosphere and its boundary with interstellar space, and provides real-time space-weather data from the Sun-Earth L1 point.

The **High-energy Ion Telescope (HIT)** measures energetic ions, from protons to heavy ions such as iron — the solar energetic particles and cosmic rays that drive radiation hazards near Earth.

## My role
I joined the HIT team in September 2025, bringing experience from Parker Solar Probe and Solar Orbiter to near-Earth energetic-particle measurements. I am a co-author of the HIT instrument paper ([Space Science Reviews 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-026-01279-6)).
