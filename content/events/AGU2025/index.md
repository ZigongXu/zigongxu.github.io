summary: Invited talk on SEP events whose mid-energy particles arrive first — what Parker Solar Probe and Solar Orbiter tell us about shock acceleration near the Sun.
abstract: |
  In most solar energetic particle (SEP) events the fastest particles arrive first. In "inverse velocity arrival" (IVA) events, particles at intermediate energies arrive before both lower- and higher-energy particles. Using Parker Solar Probe/IS⊙IS and Solar Orbiter/EPD observations, I presented a catalogue of IVA events, their energy "nose" characteristics, and what they imply about particle acceleration at evolving CME-driven shocks and transport in the inner heliosphere.
