Using Solar Orbiter/EPD/HET helium data from 2020–2022, with SOHO/EPHIN as a 1 au baseline, we measured the radial gradient of anomalous cosmic rays in the inner heliosphere: about 22% per au over 11–49 MeV/nucleon, rising to about 32% per au once the galactic cosmic-ray contribution is removed. The gradients grow as solar modulation increases.

This paper completes work I started during my PhD in Kiel.
