Data from the LND instrument on Chang'E-4 show a region in the Moon's orbit where galactic cosmic-ray protons are about 20% less intense. Earth's magnetic field obstructs cosmic rays arriving along the interplanetary magnetic field, casting a "shadow" that extends beyond the Moon's orbit — with possible benefits for timing crewed lunar activities. I am a co-first author.

📄 [Science Advances 12, eadv1908 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adv1908)
