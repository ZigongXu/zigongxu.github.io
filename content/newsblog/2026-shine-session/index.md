At the **SHINE 2026** workshop (June 29 – July 2, Madison, Wisconsin) I co-convened **Session 13: Dynamics of Particle Populations Throughout the Inner Heliosphere** with Manuel Cuesta and Richa Jain (Princeton University) and Gabriel Muro (Caltech).

The session asked how different particle populations are injected into acceleration regions in the inner heliosphere — by turbulence, reconnection, shocks or boundary layers — and how IMAP's new measurements at 1 au, combined with spacecraft at and inside 1 au, can tell these mechanisms apart.
