In most solar energetic particle events the fastest particles arrive first. In *inverse velocity arrival* (IVA) events, particles at intermediate energies — mostly 0.5–5 MeV — arrive before both slower and faster particles. Using Parker Solar Probe's IS⊙IS instruments, we identified 14 such events and built an event list for studying shock acceleration and particle transport close to the Sun.

📄 [A&A 710, A288 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202660274) · [arXiv:2602.12475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12475)
